# Portfolio Style Guide

## Voice

Use:

- first person
- practical language
- direct explanations
- enterprise-aware framing
- reusable frameworks
- product and architecture thinking

Avoid:

- third-person biography
- exaggerated claims
- motivational fluff
- buzzword-heavy writing
- excessive emojis
- confidential/internal references
- fake metrics

## Preferred Terms

Use:

- agentic AI
- MCP tools
- human-in-the-loop
- evaluation
- observability
- workflow-to-agent design
- tool-backed AI
- retail commerce workflows
- public-safe product artifacts
- reusable PM frameworks

## Claim Rules

- Do not claim production deployment unless explicitly confirmed.
- Do not mention customers.
- Do not mention revenue.
- Do not mention confidential internal architecture.
- Use "planned", "drafting", "documenting", or "building" for portfolio work in progress.

## Artifact Rules

Every repo or major document should answer:

1. What problem does this solve?
2. Who is it for?
3. When should it be used?
4. What workflow does it apply to?
5. What data/tools are required?
6. Where does human review sit?
7. How should it be evaluated?
8. How can another PM reuse it?

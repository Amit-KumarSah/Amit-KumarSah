## How I Think About AI Products

I care less about flashy demos and more about whether AI helps a real user complete a real task.

The questions I come back to:

- Who is the user?
- What decision or action are they trying to complete?
- What systems, data, and policies does the agent need?
- What should the agent be allowed to do?
- When should a human approve the action?
- How do we know the answer is accurate and useful?
- What metric proves the product is working?
